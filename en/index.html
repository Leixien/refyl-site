    In the car

    The app does the same at a glance: open it and the lowest price within a few kilometres is at the top, with distance and how recent it is. Tap Directions and Apple Maps takes you there. It works offline with the last prices downloaded.

    What it does

    • Petrol, diesel, LPG, CNG, LNG and HVO across Italy
    • Self-service or attended, by price or distance
    • Warns you when a price is old
    • Directions with Apple Maps
    • Light and dark theme, large text, VoiceOver

    What it doesn't do

    • Ads
    • Accounts
    • Data collection your location stays on the phone
    • Subscriptions

    Real data

    Prices are the ones station operators must report to the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy, updated every day. They are the same ones you just saw on the map. The app's code is public on GitHub.
