How it works

A map with the price on every station, the cheapest in green. A list sorted by price or distance, and a page with every fuel a station sells. Works offline with the last data downloaded.

What it does

  • Petrol, diesel, LPG, CNG, LNG and HVO prices
  • Self-service or attended, sorted by price or distance
  • Warns you when a price is old, so there are no surprises at the pump
  • Directions with Apple Maps
  • Light and dark theme, large text, VoiceOver

What it doesn't do

  • No ads
  • No account
  • No data collected: your location stays on the phone
  • No subscription

Official prices, open code

Prices are the ones station operators must report to the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy, updated every day. The app's code is public on GitHub.
