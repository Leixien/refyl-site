Frequently asked questions

The price in the app differs from the pump

Refyl shows the price the operator reported to the Italian ministry (MIMIT). If the operator doesn't update it, the data stays old: the app then marks it in orange with the date of the last report.

How often are prices updated?

Once a day, from MIMIT's open data. The app downloads the new file when you open it.

A station is missing

Refyl only shows stations listed in MIMIT's registry. If a station isn't there, the operator hasn't registered it or doesn't report prices.

I denied location access. How do I turn it back on?

Open Settings, then Refyl, then Location, and choose “While Using the App”. Without location the app still works: move the map to the area you need.

Does it work offline?

Yes, with the last prices downloaded. They refresh on their own when you're back online.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads.
